Privacy Policy

How VerbaDoc handles your study data

Last updated: January 2026 — Effective immediately upon publication.

The short version. VerbaDoc collects the minimum data needed to power your AI study tools: your account email, the documents you upload to generate cards, and anonymous usage analytics. We do not sell your data. We do not train third-party AI models on your documents. You can permanently delete your account at any time.

1. Who we are

VerbaDoc is an AI-powered study app for high-school and college students, available on iOS through the Apple App Store. The app is operated by an individual developer — Zoey Ding ("we", "us"). Contact: privacy@verbadoc.app.

2. Data we collect

2.1 Account data

When you sign up, we collect your email address and a salted cryptographic hash of your password. If you sign in with Apple, we receive a stable Apple user identifier and the email you choose to share (or Apple's hidden relay address). You do not need to provide your real name.

2.2 Document content you upload

When you upload a PDF, photo, YouTube link, or paste notes, we send that content to our AI generation backend (hosted on Supabase Edge Functions powered by Groq and Google Gemini) so we can return flashcards, quizzes, and study guides. The content is transmitted over HTTPS, processed in-memory by the AI model, and discarded after the response is returned. We do not use your documents to train any third-party AI model, and we configure our provider accounts to disable training-data retention where the provider offers that control.

2.3 Generated study material

The flashcards, quizzes, open-answer questions, and study guides generated from your documents are stored in our database so that you can access them across devices and so that they survive reinstalls.

2.4 Study session analytics

We collect anonymous, privacy-respecting analytics: study-session start and end timestamps, number of cards reviewed per session, streak milestones, share-action clicks, and crash reports. These do not include the content of your answers. We store a SHA-256 hash of your user identifier in the analytics log, never the identifier itself.

2.5 Subscription data (if applicable)

If you subscribe to VerbaDoc Pro, Apple processes the payment through the App Store. We receive from RevenueCat a subscription status (free / pro / expired) and the entitlement identifier. We do not see or store your credit-card number, Apple ID password, or any financial instrument.

2.6 Device & diagnostic data

If a crash occurs, Apple's MetricKit framework collects diagnostic payloads (stack trace, OS version, build identifier). We receive only the stack trace and app version; we receive no document content with crash reports.

3. Data we do NOT collect

  • Your real name, unless you provide it.
  • Your contacts, photo library (other than photos you explicitly pick), or microphone.
  • Your location, browsing history, or advertising identifiers.
  • The content of your study-session answers beyond aggregate counts.
  • Any third-party tracking pixels or cross-app identifiers.

4. How we use your data

We use the collected data only for the following purposes:

  • To provide the service. Generate your flashcards, run study sessions, sync across your devices, send the notifications you opt into.
  • To improve the product. Aggregate analytics help us understand which study modes are working and where users get stuck.
  • To respond to support requests. If you email us, we use your email to reply.
  • To comply with law. We retain the minimum data required to satisfy Apple App Store guidelines, tax obligations, and valid legal process.

5. Where your data lives

Account, decks, session logs, and study history are stored in a Supabase-hosted PostgreSQL database in the United States. Analytics are stored in your device's local Application Support directory and never uploaded to our servers in their raw form. Crash diagnostics are stored in Apple's developer portal. AI generation is processed by Groq (US region) and Google Gemini (US region) in real time; the providers do not retain content beyond request scope per their enterprise-data terms.

6. Sharing your data

We do not sell, rent, or trade your data. We share data only with the following processors, each bound by a Data Processing Agreement:

  • Supabase, Inc. — database, authentication.
  • Groq, Inc. — AI generation (Llama 3, content discarded per request).
  • Google LLC (Gemini) — AI generation fallback (content discarded per request).
  • RevenueCat, Inc. — subscription entitlement state.
  • Apple, Inc. — App Store payment processing, crash diagnostics.

We may disclose information if compelled by valid legal process, in which case we will notify you unless legally prohibited from doing so.

7. Your rights & controls

7.1 Account deletion

You can permanently delete your VerbaDoc account at any time by opening Settings → Account → Delete Account. Deletion removes your account, decks, study history, and analytics within 7 days. Backups are purged within 30 days.

7.2 Data export

Email privacy@verbadoc.app with the subject "data export" and we will provide a JSON archive of your decks, sessions, and study history within 7 days.

7.3 Notification preferences

You can disable any reminder category individually in Settings → Notifications. Disabling the OS-level permission is available through iOS Settings → VerbaDoc → Notifications.

7.4 Parental requests

If a parent or guardian wishes to review, correct, or delete a minor's data, email privacy@verbadoc.app from the email used to create the account. We respond within 7 days.

8. Children

VerbaDoc is intended for students aged 13 and older. We do not knowingly collect data from children under 13. If you believe a child under 13 has created an account, email privacy@verbadoc.app and we will delete the account within 7 days.

9. Security

All data in transit is encrypted via HTTPS / TLS 1.2+. Authentication tokens are stored in the iOS Keychain with hardware-backed encryption where available. Database access is restricted by row-level security policies. We conduct periodic access reviews.

10. International users (GDPR / CCPA)

If you are in the European Economic Area, you have the right to access, correct, port, restrict, or delete your data, and to object to processing or withdraw consent. To exercise any of these rights, contact privacy@verbadoc.app. If you are a California resident, you may request a list of the categories of personal information disclosed to third parties in the preceding calendar year.

11. Changes to this policy

We will post material changes in-app and update the "Last updated" date above. Continued use of VerbaDoc after the effective date constitutes acceptance.

12. Contact

Email privacy@verbadoc.app with any questions, concerns, or requests. We aim to respond within 7 days.
